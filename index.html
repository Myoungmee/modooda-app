MoDooDa Agent: two AIs, one better answer.

MoDooDa has Claude and ChatGPT work on your task together. Each writes its own answer, reviews the other’s, and the best parts are combined into one final answer, with disagreements shown instead of hidden.

How it works

  1. Rubric. One participant proposes what a good answer looks like. You can edit it.
  2. Independent drafts. Claude and ChatGPT each answer without seeing the other’s work.
  3. Cross-review. Each points out concrete errors, gaps and unsupported claims in the other’s draft.
  4. Revision. Each improves its own draft, accepting only feedback that holds up.
  5. One answer. The drafts are combined, checked by the other participant, and delivered with any open disagreements and things to verify.

Prefer something shorter? The Quick workflow does drafts, one review each, and the combined answer.

Ways to use MoDooDa

In your browser

Manual mode: MoDooDa writes each prompt, and you copy it into ChatGPT or Claude and paste the reply back. Works with free or paid accounts.

The phone app uses manual mode. After the first visit it also opens offline.

Desktop app

For people with a Claude or ChatGPT subscription: the desktop app will run the steps automatically through the official command-line tools, where the providers permit it. Not released yet. There is no download link until a tested installer exists.

What you need

  • A ChatGPT account and a Claude account. Free or paid both work in manual mode, within each service’s usage limits.
  • Only one of them? You can still use MoDooDa. Mark the other participant “can’t continue” and you’ll get a clearly labeled single-participant result, though without cross-review.
  • No API keys, no payment to MoDooDa.

Privacy

  • MoDooDa runs in your browser. It has no account system, no analytics and no server that receives your tasks.
  • Nothing is saved unless you choose “Save progress” or turn on auto-save.
  • When you paste text into ChatGPT or Claude, that company receives it under its own privacy policy. Private or incognito browsing doesn’t change that.

Honest limits

  • Two models agreeing is not proof. MoDooDa lists claims that need checking. Verify important facts yourself.
  • Collaboration usually catches more mistakes, but it won’t beat a single model every time.
  • Manual mode means copying and pasting: about 10 steps for the Full workflow, 5 for Quick.
