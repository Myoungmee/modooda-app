How it works

  1. Rubric. One participant proposes what a good answer looks like. You can edit it.
  2. Independent drafts. Each AI answers without seeing the others’ work.
  3. Cross-review. Each points out concrete errors, gaps and unsupported claims in the other answers.
  4. Revision. Each improves its own draft, accepting only feedback that holds up.
  5. One answer. The drafts are combined, checked by another AI, and delivered with any open disagreements and things to verify.

Prefer something shorter? The Quick workflow does drafts, one review each, and the combined answer.

How to install
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag MoDooDa Agent into Applications.
  2. The first time only: right-click (or Control-click) the app → Open → Open. On macOS 15 or later, try to open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. This beta is not yet signed by Apple, which is why that one-time step is needed. It only allows this app. Never turn off Mac security in general.
  4. If macOS says “MoDooDa Agent” is damaged (older 0.3.2 downloads), drag the app to Applications, open Terminal and paste: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/MoDooDa Agent.app" — then open the app again. This only removes the “downloaded from the internet” tag from this one app.

No install

Use MoDooDa right in your browser on any computer or phone. You copy each prompt into ChatGPT or Claude and paste the reply back.

Open MoDooDa in your browser

Updates: the apps don't update themselves yet. To update, download the latest version from this page and install it over the old one.

Your accounts: MoDooDa uses your own ChatGPT and Claude accounts. Downloads contain no accounts, passwords or keys.

What you need

  • An account with each AI you pick (for example ChatGPT and Claude). Free accounts work by copy and paste, within each service’s usage limits.
  • Only one of them? You can still use MoDooDa. Mark the other participant “can’t continue” and you’ll get a clearly labeled single-participant result, though without cross-review.
  • No API keys, no payment to MoDooDa.

Privacy

  • MoDooDa runs in your browser. It has no account system, no analytics and no server that receives your tasks.
  • Nothing is saved unless you choose “Save progress” or turn on auto-save.
  • When you paste text into ChatGPT or Claude, that company receives it under its own privacy policy. Private or incognito browsing doesn’t change that.

Honest limits

  • Two models agreeing is not proof. MoDooDa lists claims that need checking. Verify important facts yourself.
  • Collaboration usually catches more mistakes, but it won’t beat a single model every time.
  • Manual mode means copying and pasting: about 10 steps for the Full workflow, 5 for Quick.
